# AGENTS.md - Workspace Rules for REKEN Landing Page

## Absolute Positioning Rule
This project is exclusively for marketing the **REKEN Web Dashboard SaaS & Operating System** for vehicle rental and fleet businesses.
- **NEVER** write content advertising consumer car rentals, tourist packages, airport shuttles, or daily self-drive car rental.
- **ALWAYS** write content framing REKEN as a B2B Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) and Enterprise Management System.
- Target customers: Rental owners, fleet managers, and tour operators across Indonesia.

## Primary Modules to Feature
1. Manajemen Rental (Booking timeline, anti-conflict)
2. Manajemen Customer / CRM (Data pelanggan, blacklist, loyalitas)
3. Manajemen Fleet / Armada (Inventaris mobil/motor/bus, status, servis)
4. Manajemen Keuangan & Invoice (Deposit, DP, kas, kalkulasi dinamis)
5. WhatsApp AI CS Copilot (Otomatisasi chat 24/7, booking bot)
6. Dashboard AI & Analytics (Tingkat okupansi armada, insight)
7. Google Calendar Sync (Sinkronisasi kalender tim otomatis)
8. GPS Tracking & Telematika Armada (Pelacakan rute & anti-penggelapan)
9. Laporan Otomatis (Ekspor CSV/PDF rekap keuangan & utilitas)

## Design System
- Light Modern SaaS Aesthetic: Clean, airy, crisp typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), blue-teal accents (`#0284c7`, `#06b6d4`, `#1e40af`), deep slate contrasts.
- High-fidelity dashboard screenshots embedded as UI proof.
